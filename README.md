# Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd · UK 16939677)

**We measure AI systems against the rules that govern them, sign the result (Ed25519), and re-attest when measurements change.**
Not a certifier. Not an enforcer. No accreditation chain — an independent measurement instrument.

### Public grid (15 slots)
- **13 live axes** — governance, safety, provenance, continuity, conformance, openness, machinery, care, cross-reality, detector-interop, art5-safeguard, swarm, affect.
- **gspc_jail** — quarantine slot for models that fail critical thresholds (13 Aug floor; empty on the 12 Aug stamp).
- **Slot 15** — reserved, unnamed.

Affect is **MEASURED** (n=41), counsel-pending — not a legal verdict.

### Living board
Scores live at **[councilof.ai/api/gspc](https://councilof.ai/api/gspc)** (same payload on csoai.org). Frontends read from this API; they never keep their own scores.

**12 Aug 2026 stamp:** 13 axes × 19 models · 819 items · UNSIGNED.

### Verification
Free to verify: **[councilof.ai/gspc-verify](https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify)**

Cards are Ed25519-signed + time-anchored (OpenTimestamps against the Bitcoin calendar); anyone can recompute from the published rows.

### The method
Deterministic gold labels · no model judges another model · unparsed counts incorrect · nothing quoted below n≥30 · every published number recomputable from its rows · corrections published, never silently edited.
