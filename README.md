# Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd · UK 16939677)

**We measure AI systems against the rules that govern them, sign the result (Ed25519), and publish what we cannot yet measure.**
Not a certifier. Not an enforcer. No accreditation chain — an independent measurement instrument.

### What's public and verified
- **13 GSPC axis benchmarks** — governance, safety, provenance, continuity, conformance, openness, machinery, care, cross-reality, detector-interop, art5-safeguard, swarm, and **affect** (emotional/manipulation safety, EU AI Act Art 5 + Art 50).
  → banks on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/csoai) and [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/nicktempleman) (13/13 on both).
- **Durable measurement boards** — [`csoai/gspc-boards`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/csoai/gspc-boards): deterministic multi-model scoring, Wilson intervals, tail-risk (CVaR + correlated-failure), and honest **UNMEASURED** where the floor (n≥30) isn't met.
- **Layer-0 compliance MCPs** — EU AI Act (410 verbatim articles), DORA, CRA Annex IV, NIS2, C2PA/Art 50 watermarking. See pinned repos.

### The method
Deterministic gold labels · no model judges another model · unparsed counts incorrect · nothing quoted below n≥30 · every published number recomputable from its rows · corrections published, never silently edited.
